GOOD TO KNOW

Questions from
brand teams.

What campaign formats can we discuss?

Possible areas include brand promotion, product sampling, creator content, referrals, surveys, research and app testing. Confirm current availability with the Funngro team.

Can campaigns be tailored to our audience?

Share your intended audience, locations, eligibility requirements and objectives with the team. Feasibility and targeting depend on current platform capabilities and applicable rules.

What should our campaign brief include?

Include the objective, target audience, deliverables, timeline, review criteria, usage rights, compensation terms if applicable and any required disclosures or safeguards.

How do we get started?

Use the official Funngro contact page to discuss your brief and confirm current campaign options.
