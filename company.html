Good to know

Questions from brand teams.

Clear information helps everyone make a confident decision.

What can brands do with Funngro?

Brands can explore promotion, sampling, creator content, referrals, surveys, app testing, ideation, sales support, loyalty and campus ambassador campaigns.

Can campaigns be tailored to a target audience?

Campaign planning can consider relevant audience cohorts, locations and campaign requirements, subject to current platform capabilities and applicable rules.

How do we start a brand campaign?

Share your brand, audience and objective with the Funngro team through the official contact page or hello@funngro.com.

What should a campaign brief include?

A useful brief explains the objective, target cohort, deliverables, timeline, review criteria, usage rights and any applicable disclosures.

Have a brief worth putting into motion?

Tell us what you want to achieve. We’ll help you explore the right campaign format.
