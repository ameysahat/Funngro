Your safety and privacy matter. Before joining any opportunity, understand who is offering it, what is expected and how your information will be used.

01

Check age eligibility and any parent or guardian consent requirements.

02

04

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before
you begin?

Who can participate?

Eligibility can depend on current Funngro rules, age, location and the specific opportunity. Check the official platform terms before registering or applying.

Do I need experience?

Requirements vary by project. Read each opportunity carefully and apply only when you can meet its stated expectations.

How do rewards or payments work?

Any reward, payment amount, timing and eligibility are determined by the applicable project terms and the official Funngro platform. Review those terms before accepting work.

What if I need help?

Talk with a parent or trusted adult and use the official Funngro support and contact channels for platform questions.
