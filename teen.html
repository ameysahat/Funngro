Good to know

Questions before you begin.

Clear information helps everyone make a confident decision.

What kind of projects can teens explore?

Opportunities may include content creation, brand promotion, referrals, sampling, surveys, influencer campaigns and app testing. Availability varies.

Do I need previous experience?

Not every opportunity requires experience. Read each project brief carefully and choose work that matches your current skills.

How do I receive rewards?

Reward eligibility, approval and payout timing depend on the individual campaign and Funngro’s current terms. Review the details before participating.

Who can join Funngro?

Age, verification and account requirements apply. Check the current official terms and involve a parent or guardian where required.
